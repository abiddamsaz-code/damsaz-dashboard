1. Overview & General Data Protection

Damsaz Technologies ("we", "our", or "us"), founded by Abid Damsaz Lakhair, respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal data of our users across all our web, mobile, and educational software applications.

This Privacy Policy explains how data is collected, used, processed, and protected across our products. We adhere strictly to applicable privacy standards, including Google Play Developer Policies, COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act), and GDPR principles.

2. Hisaab – Personal Finance Tracker

Offline-First Financial Privacy Guarantee

100% Local Storage: HiSaab is designed as an offline-first financial ledger. All your income records, expense logs, account balances (cash, bank, wallet), transaction tags, and categories are stored locally on your device in an encrypted Room SQLite database.

Biometric & Security Data: HiSaab supports biometric unlock (PIN and Fingerprint authentication). Biometric validation is processed exclusively by your Android device hardware security module via official Android BiometricPrompt APIs. We do not store, access, or transmit your fingerprint or PIN data.

Cloud Sync (Optional): If you choose to enable background cloud sync, data is transmitted exclusively over encrypted HTTPS connections to our secure server endpoints. Cloud sync is disabled by default and can be turned off anytime in Settings.

Data Export & Deletion: You retain full ownership of your data. You can export a full JSON backup of your records to your device storage at any time or reset/delete all local database records instantly.

🐥

3. Little Explorer – Toddler Educational Flashcards

COPPA & Families Policy Compliant • 100% Kid Safe
✓ CERTIFIED: ZERO PERSONAL DATA COLLECTION FOR CHILDREN

Target Audience & Children's Privacy: Little Explorer is an educational flashcard Android application built for toddlers and young children (Ages 2–8). We comply fully with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policy requirements.

Zero Data Collection: We do NOT collect, store, request, or share any personal information from children or parents. The app does not ask for names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, device identifiers, or microphone recordings.

No Ads & No In-App Purchases: Little Explorer contains zero third-party advertisements, zero tracking SDKs, zero analytics code, and zero hidden in-app purchases. It operates 100% offline once downloaded.

📚

4. Mind Forge – MCQ Testing & Assessment Platform

Educational Assessment Data

Account Information: When registered as a teacher or student on Mind Forge, we collect basic account credentials (name, email address, password hashes using industry-standard bcrypt encryption) to authenticate access.

Test Results & Analytics: MCQ test submissions, score reports, time per question, and subject analytics are stored to provide performance feedback for students and grading tools for teachers.

Data Protection: Educational test data is confidential and never sold, rented, or shared with third-party advertisers.

🏢

5. CCMS & SMS – Institution Management Systems

School & Coaching Centre Administration

Institutional Data: CCMS (Centre Management System) and SMS (School Management System) process student enrolment records, attendance logs, fee receipts, class timetables, and academic report cards.

Role-Based Access Control: Access to institutional data is strictly restricted based on authenticated user roles (Super Admin, Principal, Teacher, Student, Parent). Data belongs entirely to the subscribing institution.
